Methodology

What this experiment does—and does not claim.

01

Savings

Monthly compounding using the annualized CBN savings benchmark divided by 12.

02

Fixed deposits

The selected tenor rate is locked at term start, accrues simple interest, and rolls at maturity using the rate then available.

03

Inflation

Real balances use the NBS headline All Items CPI on the pre-2025 basis, expressed in the purchasing power of your chosen starting month.

04

Foreign exchange

USD equivalents use the CBN monthly official/reference NGN-per-USD series—not the parallel market.

Important: this is a benchmark simulation. It excludes bank-specific fees, taxes, minimum balances, early-withdrawal rules and product-specific compounding. Missing fixed-deposit rates are never filled or guessed; affected simulations stop and tell you where the missing rate occurs.

Data: CBN Money Market Indicators, CBN official/reference exchange rates, and NBS headline CPI. Historical range: Jan 2014–Dec 2024. The historical CPI module stays on the pre-2025 basis and does not splice the rebased 2025 series into the sample.
