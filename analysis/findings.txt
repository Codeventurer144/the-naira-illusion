THE NAIRA ILLUSION — STAGE 2 ANALYTICAL FINDINGS
Coverage: January 2014 to December 2024
Default analytical balance: NGN 1,000,000

MODEL CONVENTIONS
- Savings compounds monthly using the annualized CBN savings benchmark divided by 12.
- Fixed deposits lock the tenor rate at term start, accrue simple interest, and roll at maturity.
- Fixed-deposit simulations stop if a required rollover-month rate is unavailable; no imputation is used.
- Real balances are expressed in start-month naira using NBS headline CPI.
- USD equivalents use the CBN official/reference NGN per USD series.

STORY-READY FINDINGS
1. The CBN savings benchmark failed to beat inflation in every valid rolling 12-month window in the 2014-2024 sample (0 of 120 positive real windows).
2. Fixed deposits occasionally preserved purchasing power, but only in limited periods: 3m had 13 positive real windows, 6m had 15, and 12m had 8.
3. The most severe savings purchasing-power loss was Jun-2023 to Jun-2024: about -21.20% real despite a positive nominal return of about +5.74%.
4. The sharpest 12-month USD-equivalent deterioration for the savings benchmark was Mar-2023 to Mar-2024: about -67.91%, even after interest.
5. Over Jan-2014 to Dec-2024, NGN 1,000,000 on the savings benchmark grows to roughly NGN 1.50m nominally, but has only about NGN 266k of Jan-2014 purchasing power and about USD 968 at the Dec-2024 official/reference rate.
6. A rolling 12-month fixed-deposit strategy grows the same NGN 1,000,000 to about NGN 2.42m nominally over the full sample, yet still ends around NGN 427k in Jan-2014 purchasing power: a real loss of about 57.31%.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION NOTE
These are benchmark simulations, not reconstructions of a particular retail bank account. Taxes, fees, minimum balances, bank-specific compounding, early-withdrawal rules, and parallel-market FX are excluded.
